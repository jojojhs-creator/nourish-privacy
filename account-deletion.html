Delete Your VitaIQ Account and Data

VitaIQ, provided by VitaIQ, lets you permanently delete your account and associated data from inside the app or request deletion if you cannot access the app.

Delete from the VitaIQ app

  1. Open VitaIQ and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account & All Data.
  4. Tap it and confirm both deletion prompts.

This permanently deletes your Supabase account and the VitaIQ data stored locally on that device, including meal plans and logs, goals, weight history, progress photos, optional cycle tracking, saved recipes, grocery lists, reminders, and settings. This cannot be undone.

Request deletion without the app

Email support.nourishlabs@gmail.com from the address connected to your VitaIQ account with the subject VitaIQ Account Deletion Request. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Verified requests are processed within 30 days.

Delete specific data without deleting your account

You can delete individual local entries directly in VitaIQ. To request deletion of specific server-side data while keeping your account, email support.nourishlabs@gmail.com from your account email with the subject VitaIQ Data Deletion Request and identify the data you want removed. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Verified requests are processed within 30 days.

Data deleted or retained

  • Deleted: Supabase account identity, email, display name, account profile, server-side usage records, AI safety reports linked to the account, and VitaIQ data stored locally on the device where in-app deletion is completed.
  • Not automatically removed from an unavailable device: local app data on a lost or inaccessible device. Remove VitaIQ from that device when you regain access.
  • May be retained: limited records required for fraud prevention, payment reconciliation, security, or legal obligations, for only as long as necessary.

Subscriptions

Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel separately in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → VitaIQ.
