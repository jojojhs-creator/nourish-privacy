Delete Your VitaIQ Account

You can permanently delete your VitaIQ account and all associated data at any time. Because VitaIQ stores your data locally on your device, deletion is immediate and complete.

Delete from within the app (recommended)

  1. Open VitaIQ
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll to the bottom
  4. Tap "Delete Account & All Data"
  5. Confirm twice

This permanently removes your account, meal logs, meal plans, weight history, progress photos, cycle tracking data, grocery lists, reminders, and all settings from your device. It cannot be undone.

What is deleted

All personal data: account details, nutrition logs, health logs, photos, and preferences. An anonymous trial-start timestamp (containing no personal information) is retained solely to prevent free-trial abuse.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — subscriptions are managed by Google Play. To cancel: open the Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → VitaIQ → Cancel.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app (e.g., lost device), email us at jojo_jhs@yahoo.com with the subject "VitaIQ Account Deletion Request" and the email address you signed up with. We will remove any subscription records associated with your account (held by RevenueCat) within 30 days. Note that on-device data can only be removed by uninstalling the app from the device.
