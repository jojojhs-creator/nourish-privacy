Privacy Policy — VitaIQ

Last updated: July 8, 2026

VitaIQ ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the VitaIQ mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

The short version: your data lives on your device. VitaIQ stores your account, meals, health logs, and photos locally on your phone — we do not operate servers that hold your personal data. The only data that leaves your device is what is needed to power specific features (AI answers, barcode lookups, subscription management), described below.

Information the App Stores on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only. It is not uploaded to us and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Account information: Your name and email address. Your password is stored only as a salted cryptographic hash — never in plain text.
  • Nutrition data: Meals you log, meal plans, custom meals, saved recipes, grocery lists, and water intake.
  • Body data: Weight history, body measurements, and progress photos. Progress photos are stored only on your device and are never uploaded automatically.
  • Cycle tracking data (optional): If you enable Women's Health tracking, your cycle logs, symptoms, and notes are stored on your device only and are never transmitted anywhere.
  • Health data: With your explicit permission, the app reads steps, heart rate, active calories, and sleep data via Android Health Connect (or Apple Health on iOS) to display your daily activity. This data stays on your device.
  • App preferences: Your settings, goals, and reminders.

Data That Leaves Your Device (and Why)

  • AI Assistant & Recipe Generator (Google Gemini): When you use the AI chat or generate a recipe, your messages together with relevant nutrition context (such as your daily calorie and macro goals, remaining calories, and a summary of today's logged meals) are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. If you explicitly request AI feedback on a progress photo, a compressed copy of that photo is sent for analysis. This data is used only to generate your answer and is subject to Google's privacy policy. We do not send your name, email, or password to the AI.
  • Barcode scanning (Open Food Facts): When you scan a food barcode, the barcode number is sent to the Open Food Facts database to retrieve nutrition information. No personal data is included.
  • Subscriptions (RevenueCat & Google Play): Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat and Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details — payment is handled entirely by the app store. RevenueCat receives your sign-in email solely to associate your subscription with your account so it can be restored across devices.

Device Permissions

  • Camera: Used to scan food barcodes and to take progress or profile photos. Photos are stored on your device.
  • Notifications: Used for meal, water, and custom reminders. These are local notifications scheduled on your device — we do not send remote push notifications.
  • Physical activity / Health Connect: Used, with your permission, to read step counts and activity data for your dashboard.
  • Microphone: The app requests this permission for planned voice-input features. The microphone is not currently accessed by any feature.

Health Data & Google Health Connect

VitaIQ's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only access the health data necessary to display your activity and provide nutrition insights. Health Connect data is not shared with third parties and is not used for advertising.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect financial or payment card information, precise location data, or your contacts. VitaIQ contains no advertising and no advertising trackers, and we do not use analytics services that profile you.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Data is shared with the service providers listed above only to the extent required to deliver the feature you are using.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored on your device, you are in control: you can delete individual entries in the app, delete your entire account in-app (Profile → Delete Account & All Data), and uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. See our account deletion page for full instructions. To remove your subscription record from RevenueCat, or for any other deletion request, contact us at the address below and we will process it within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

VitaIQ is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, through an in-app notice.
