VitaIQ for iOS — Support

Help with your VitaIQ account, nutrition tracking, meal planning, home workouts and App Store subscriptions.

VitaIQ for iOS is for adults aged 18 or older. If prompted, save your age in whole years for your current account before accessing app features. The age check is self-reported, not identity verification. Support, recovery, sign-out and account-deletion options remain available.

Contact support

Email hello.vitaiq@gmail.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, VitaIQ version and the steps that led to the problem. You may include a screenshot with private details removed. Never send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details or private progress photos.

Sign-in and password reset

Use the password-reset option on the sign-in screen if you cannot remember your password. If the app asks you to confirm your email, check the inbox and spam folder for the account you registered. Contact support if the confirmation or reset message does not arrive.

Subscriptions and restoring access

VitaIQ Pro offers monthly, three-month and yearly auto-renewing App Store subscriptions. Your localized recurring price and billing period are shown before purchase. To restore an existing purchase, use Restore Purchases on the subscription screen while signed in to the Apple Account used for that purchase.

Manage or cancel through Apple subscription settings. Deleting VitaIQ or your VitaIQ account does not cancel an Apple subscription. For Apple refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

AI features and sharing choices

AI chat, recipe generation and optional progress-photo AI feedback require an eligible adult account. You can update your age under Profile → Nutrition Goals → Recalculate. Text and photo sharing require separate permission. Change those permissions under Profile → AI Data Sharing. Declining AI sharing does not prevent eligible adults from using the app’s other tracking features.

Account and data deletion

In VitaIQ, open Profile → Delete Account & All Data to request account deletion. If you cannot access your account or need help with a privacy request, contact the support address above. Subscription cancellation is a separate Apple action.
