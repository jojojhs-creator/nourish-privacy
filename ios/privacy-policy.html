VitaIQ for iOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy describes how VitaIQ for iOS handles account information, nutrition and wellness entries, subscriptions and optional AI requests. Contact support.nourishlabs@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Account and authentication

Creating an account, signing in, verifying your email and resetting your password use Supabase Authentication. Your email, password and account details needed for those operations are sent to Supabase. Your account identifier and authentication records are held by that service. The app keeps your signed-in session on the device; your password is not stored as a local account password hash.

Data saved on your device

The app saves your profile and goals, meal logs, meal plans, custom meals, saved recipes, grocery lists, water and weight logs, workout records, reminders, settings and progress photos locally. Optional cycle tracking entries are also stored locally. This is separate from the authentication and service-provider data described in this policy. Device or operating-system backups may preserve local app data according to your device settings.

Optional AI: Google Gemini through Supabase

AI sharing requires your explicit permission before a request is sent. You can cancel without sending data and keep using the app’s other tracking features. Text/recipe permission and photo-feedback permission are separate and are saved for the signed-in account on this device. Revoke them in Profile → AI Data Sharing. Revocation affects future requests and cannot recall data already sent.

  • Chat: your message and recent conversation (up to 12 turns), your profile name and goal, calorie and macro goals, remaining calories and macros, today’s meal summary, water intake and workout calorie total are sent to Google Gemini through VitaIQ’s Supabase service to generate personalized replies.
  • Recipes: your selected dietary requirements and nutrition goals are sent through the same service to Google Gemini to generate a recipe.
  • Photo feedback: only when you request it and allow photo sharing, the selected progress photo, tracking week, nutrition goals and weight change are sent through the same service to Google Gemini for progress feedback. A photo is transmitted; local storage of the original does not mean the AI copy stays on the device.
  • Your account ID authenticates requests to VitaIQ’s Supabase service and is used for service usage limits. Do not include information in a message or photo that you do not want processed by these providers. Provider processing and retention are subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices; VitaIQ does not promise that a submitted request or photo has zero retention.

Subscriptions and other services

  • Apple and RevenueCat process App Store purchases and subscription status. VitaIQ identifies your RevenueCat customer using your sign-in email so purchases can be associated with your account. RevenueCat receives customer identifiers and purchase/subscription information. Payment details are handled by Apple; VitaIQ does not receive your payment card number.
  • Barcode lookup sends the scanned barcode to Open Food Facts to retrieve food information. The barcode lookup does not need your account email or nutrition log.
  • If you contact support, the information you choose to include is processed to respond to your request. If you explicitly submit an AI-content report, its content, reason and context may be retained with your account ID for review.

Permissions and health features

  • Camera and photo-library access support barcode scanning and photos you choose to take or select. OS permission to access a photo is separate from permission to send it for AI feedback.
  • Notifications support reminders scheduled on your device.
  • Apple Health synchronization is currently unavailable. VitaIQ does not read HealthKit data in this version.

Sharing and advertising

VitaIQ uses the providers described above to deliver app features. It does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising. The app does not request precise location or contacts.

Retention, deletion and your choices

You can remove individual entries and photos in the app and request account deletion from Profile → Delete Account & All Data. Account deletion must complete with the authenticated service before the app clears the local account. Deleting the app or its local data alone does not delete your Supabase account, cancel an Apple subscription or remove records held by service providers. Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple’s subscription settings. Apple, RevenueCat, Supabase and Google may retain information under their own policies or applicable obligations. Contact support for privacy requests that are not available in the app.

Children and policy updates

VitaIQ is not directed at children under 13. Changes to this policy are reflected in the update date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
